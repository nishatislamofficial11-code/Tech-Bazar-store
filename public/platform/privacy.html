1. Who we are

Cloud Commerce ("we", "us") is a Bangladeshi software-as-a-service platform that lets businesses ("Merchants") build and run their own online shops ("Shops"). People who buy from those Shops are "Customers". You can reach us at a message to our Facebook page "Cloud Commerce".

For a Merchant's account and subscription, Cloud Commerce decides how data is used. For the data of a Shop's Customers (orders, addresses, chats), the Merchant decides how it is used and Cloud Commerce processes it on the Merchant's behalf. Questions about a particular Shop's orders should go to that Shop first.

2. Information we collect

Merchants

  • Account details: name, mobile number (verified by a one-time code), e-mail, password (stored only as a secure hash), business name and type.
  • Shop content: products, prices, images, pages, settings, and the credentials a Merchant enters for payment gateways, couriers, SMS or AI services.
  • Subscription and billing records.

Customers of a Shop

  • Name, mobile number, delivery address, order details and order history; an account e-mail and password hash if the Customer creates an account.
  • Payment status and transaction references returned by the payment gateway. We never receive card numbers or mobile-wallet PINs.
  • Messages and photos sent to a Shop's chat assistant or to a connected Facebook Page, Messenger, Instagram or WhatsApp account.

Automatically

  • Technical data such as IP address, device and browser type, pages visited, and a random device identifier used to remember a chat and to stop fraudulent or abusive orders.
  • Browser storage (local storage and cookies) to keep you signed in, remember a cart and keep a chat open. Shops may also use Facebook Pixel or TikTok Pixel if the Merchant turns them on.

3. How we use information

  • To provide the platform: run Shops, take and deliver orders, process payments, send order and account messages.
  • To answer Customers through the AI assistant and, when needed, pass the conversation to the Shop's staff.
  • To keep the service safe: verify sign-ups, detect fake orders, block abuse, keep backups.
  • To support Merchants and improve the platform. We do not sell personal data and do not use Customers' data to advertise to them on our own behalf.

4. Facebook, Messenger, Instagram and WhatsApp data

When a Merchant (or Cloud Commerce for its own page) connects a Facebook Page, Instagram account or WhatsApp number, we receive the messages, comments, sender's public name and Page-scoped ID that people send to that account. We use this data only to read and reply to those conversations, show them in the Shop's inbox, and take orders the person asks for. We do not sell it, use it for unrelated advertising, or share it with anyone except the service providers in section 5 that are needed to produce a reply. Disconnecting the account stops new data from arriving.

5. Who we share information with

  • The Merchant whose Shop you use (they see your orders and chats with their Shop).
  • Service providers acting for us or the Merchant: hosting and database (Cloudflare, Supabase), image storage (Cloudinary), payment gateways (bKash, SSLCommerz, EPS and others the Merchant selects), couriers (Steadfast, Pathao), SMS (BulkSMSBD), notifications (Telegram, if the Merchant enables it) and AI model providers that generate chat replies (Anthropic, and where selected OpenAI, Google, Groq or OpenRouter). They may only use the data to provide their service.
  • Authorities, when required by Bangladeshi law or to protect people from fraud or harm.

6. How long we keep it

Account and Shop data are kept while the account is active. Order records may be kept for as long as the Merchant needs them for accounting and legal reasons. Chat histories are kept so staff can follow up; Merchants can delete conversations. After an account is closed we delete or anonymise its data within 90 days, except backups, which roll over within 30 days, and records we must keep by law.

7. Security

Data is sent over HTTPS, passwords are stored as hashes, each Shop can only see its own data, and secret keys are hidden from the public. No system is perfectly secure, so please keep your password private.

8. Your choices and rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your personal data, or to stop receiving messages. Merchants can edit most data in their admin panel. Customers can contact the Shop or us.

9. Data deletion

To delete your data, including data received through Facebook, Messenger, Instagram or WhatsApp:

  1. Send a message to the Cloud Commerce Facebook page or contact us at the Cloud Commerce Facebook page with the subject "Delete my data".
  2. Include the phone number, e-mail or Facebook name you used, and the Shop name if it concerns a Shop.
  3. We confirm the request and delete the data within 30 days, and tell you when it is done. If you used a Shop, we also inform that Merchant.

10. Children

The platform is not meant for children under 13, and Merchants must not knowingly collect their data.

11. Changes

We may update this policy. The effective date above shows the latest version; important changes will be announced on the platform or our Facebook page.

12. Contact

Message the Cloud Commerce Facebook page.
